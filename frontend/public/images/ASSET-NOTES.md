# ZELVORA fleet artwork

The seven vehicle SVGs in this directory were authored for this project during Phase 4.
They contain original paths, gradients and studio compositions, not third-party photographs,
manufacturer logos or embedded/external image resources. They follow the approved ZELVORA
palette, scale from a 4:3 (800 × 600) viewBox and require no image/font CDN.

They are representative category illustrations, **not photographs of the named rental vehicles**.
Vehicle specifications, availability and price come from the API, not the artwork.
No claim of manufacturer endorsement or real operational fleet photography is intended.

The existing project/repository licensing terms govern their use. No additional third-party
licence, attribution obligation or stock-image download was introduced.
